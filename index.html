Amile’s Drive [RUSTENBURG]

A cherry-red roadster heading past the Moon toward Mars. Cold-gas thrusters under the car fire it upward like a rocket; rotate it to aim the push, and flip it right round for points.

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger game.

  • Space ↑ or hold the game: thrusters
  • ← → or A D: rotate
  • P pause
  • M sound

The thrusters push out of the car's underside, so tilting the nose down drives you forward and tilting it back pulls you back; let go of the rotate keys and the car levels itself. A full flip scores 100, charge cells 25, and a violet bubble absorbs one hit. On a touchscreen, use the two round buttons in the corner to rotate. Everything is drawn inside this one page, so it plays with no internet connection.
